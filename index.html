Ingredient checker

pore

Scan, photograph or type a skincare or makeup product. It shows which ingredients may clog pores, colour-coded and easy to read at a glance.

Suited to
Anyone choosing skincare or makeup
How it works
Reads the barcode, the pack or a typed list. Checks every ingredient against a list I researched from lab studies. Finds missing products on the web by itself.
Made with
JavaScript, Vercel, Claude, Open Beauty Facts
