Newsbot

Personal tool, running daily

Most news apps are built to keep you scrolling. I wanted the opposite. Newsbot sends me one short message every morning with only the big stories on topics I care about, then stops. No feed, no notifications all day.

What arrives each morning

newsbot08:00
Today's big news
Ferrari revealed a new limited-edition model at its launch event. read
Claude AI Anthropic released a new version of Claude. read
Apple announced a new product ahead of the holidays. read

An illustrative example. On quiet days it just says nothing big happened.

How it works

  1. Collects the last day's headlines for each topic I follow
  2. Filters them with Claude, which keeps only real, big news and drops rumours, reviews and repeats
  3. Writes one plain sentence per story, at most seven a day
  4. Sends the digest to Telegram and remembers what it already sent
  5. Runs itself every morning on GitHub Actions, so my laptop can stay off
